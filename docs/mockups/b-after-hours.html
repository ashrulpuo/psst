After Hours

The same playful product, lit from below. Deep ink canvas, aurora bleed, glass notes with a neon edge, and one signature gesture: hold to whisper — press, watch the ring fill, release to send. It's the version you open at 1am when the good messages happen.

Feel

Dark-first, light is the sequel. Depth comes from glow and edge light, never from a grey box. One accent per moment: cyan = live / private, violet = action, pink = anonymous sender.

Motion & micro-interactions

⏳ hold to whisper

Press anywhere on the orb: the cyan ring fills over 700ms with a haptic tick at 50%, the core squashes to 0.92, and releasing early snaps back with a 1.06 overshoot. Only a full hold sends — an accidental tap can never post a confession.

🌊 aurora drift

Two blurred gradient orbs drift 6–10px over 12s behind the content. It's the only continuous animation, and it never touches text contrast (orbs sit at 0.18–0.45 alpha behind a solid panel).

📡 the receipt

After send, three mono rows stagger in 80ms apart and the trace code types itself in 240ms. Then a single haptic "success". No confetti — the calm is the reward at this hour.

💬 bubbles with weight

Their glass bubble enters from 12px below at 0.96 scale; yours enters from the right with a 1.02 overshoot. Read receipts shift cyan → white rather than swapping glyphs.

🎚 live LED

Unread rows carry a cyan LED that breathes every 2.2s. Opening a thread drains the LED out over 300ms and flips the badge to "read", in sync with the realtime read receipt.

♿ Guardrails

Text #F4F1FF on #07060C is 17.8:1; muted #A49BC4 is 7.4:1; violet buttons use #5426E8 so white stays above 4.5:1. Glow never carries meaning alone — every state also has a label. All loops stop for reduced motion, and dark mode still ships a light palette for daytime.

Signature touches

🧿 the orb

The receiver has no photo on the send screen — just a glowing orb with their initial. It makes the sender's screen feel like a private channel, and avoids the awkward "which photo did they pick" question.

🔢 trace codes

Every send gets a 4-character trace ("4F2A"). It's not identifiable — it's a comfort object. It also gives support a handle if someone reports a message.

🌗 day mode

Same layout, inverted tokens: warm off-white canvas, violet-to-pink beacon, glass becomes paper. The neon is replaced by saturated gradient edges — playful stays, the mood lightens.

Watch out

Glow fatigue

Nine glowing things is noise. Keep glow for: live state, the send action, and the link beacon. Everything else earns contrast from borders.

Hold discoverability

A hold-only send is unusual — pair it with the visible "hold to send" label and allow a normal tap on the text mode's send button. Never make hold the only path.

Battery & blur

Backdrop blur + big blurs are expensive on low-end Android. Cap at two blurred layers, drop them under 360px width or when the device reports reduced motion.
