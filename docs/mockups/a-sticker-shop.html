Sticker Shop

Psst as a table full of stickers and marker pens: cream paper canvas, candy-gradient note cards, chunky ink-outlined buttons that physically press in, and emoji that pop when you tap. Loud enough to screenshot, cheap enough to feel casual — the app is a toy, not a tool.

Bricolage Grotesque 800 Display · 31 / 28 / 22 / 17 · tracking −0.03em Nunito 600–800 Body · 16.5 message, 14.5 copy, 11.5 meta

Feel

Ink outlines, hard offset shadows, 3px borders, 18–28px radii. Every tappable thing moves down 4–5px and loses its shadow on press — the tactile "keyboard key" effect.

Signature touches & motion rules

🫸 The press

Every button sits on a 4–6px hard ink shadow. Touch it and it drops onto the page (translateY + shadow to 0) in 120ms — no scale, no fade. Release and it springs back at 220ms with a tiny overshoot.

🎉 Send = celebration

Tapping send fires 12 emoji (💌✨🤍🎉) outward from the button with random arcs, the card scales 0.96 → 1.04 → 1, and the receipt row slides up 200ms later. Capped at 900ms; skipped entirely for reduced motion.

👆 Swipe the stack

Notes drag horizontally with the finger (no scripted animation), snap open past 72px to reveal a full-card "hide 🙈" layer, snap back otherwise. Vertical scroll always wins the gesture if the first movement is vertical.

✨ Stickers are alive

Avatar floats ±5px on a 4.5s loop; sparkles wiggle out of phase; the "ask me anything" tape label sits at 7°. Motion is slow and shallow so it never competes with reading.

😄 Tap-to-react

Tap a bubble → the reaction chip pops in with a 1.25 overshoot, and the bubble nudges 2px. Long-press opens block / report, which stay in the ⋮ menu too — never hidden behind gesture only.

♿ Guardrails

Ink on paper is 14.5:1; white on the pink button is 4.9:1; white on gradients always has a dark end-stop. 44px minimum targets, focus rings are 3px ink, all motion honors prefers-reduced-motion.

What changes from today

Kept

  • Warm cream canvas, one accent family, Nunito display type
  • Story-gradient cards and the two-pane desktop shell
  • Guest-first flow: send → optional sign-in → chat

Dialled up

  • Cards tilt, buttons have hard shadows and real press feedback
  • Prompt chips, emoji quick-row, reactions, swipe-to-hide
  • Wordmark with a winking sticker instead of a plain wordmark

Watch out

  • Bright gradients + big shadows tempt low contrast — every card keeps a dark gradient end
  • Too many animations can feel cheap; cap at one celebratory moment per action
  • Desktop needs the tilt toned down (‑0.5° max) or it reads as broken layout
