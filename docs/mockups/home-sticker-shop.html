Homepage · Theme A · Sticker Shop

Two ways to land at Psst

Today / just redirects: signed in → your link page, stranger → the sign-in card. That makes the front door a gate. These two homepages open with the product instead — a note someone already sent — and let a visitor choose a door without reading a paragraph first.

Who lands here

A stranger from a story link who has never heard of Psst, on a phone, in a hurry. They should understand “send an anonymous note” in one glance and be able to act in one tap.

Two doors, one primary

Get my link (no account) is the main action. Sign in is there for returning people and stays visually secondary — never a wall.

Same tokens as the app

Paper, ink, hot pink, lime, Bricolage 800, 2.5px borders, hard shadows, press-down buttons. Nothing new to build: it reuses AppButton, the sticker card and the coin-flip pop.

What changes on the real /

Routing

  • / renders this homepage instead of redirecting.
  • Signed-in users skip it: the guard sends them to /link so the app still opens on their link.
  • Get my link → /get-link (already exists, no account).
  • Sign in → /login (already exists).

Copy, kept short

“say the thing. no name attached.” / “get my link · free” / “I have an account — sign in”. The privacy line stays one sentence and names exactly who can see what.

Build cost

One new page file, one route, reuse of AppButton, the sticker gradient helper and the existing tokens. No server change: /get-link and /login already do the work.

Motion & a11y

Staggered rise on load only; the drifting notes stop under prefers-reduced-motion. Both CTAs are real links in DOM order, 56px tall, ink focus rings, and the sample notes are aria-hidden so a screen reader hears the pitch, not the demo.
